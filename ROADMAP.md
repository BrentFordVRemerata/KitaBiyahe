# Roadmap proposals

These are ideas for owner review, not approved work, scheduled releases, or implemented features. Any future change should preserve the financial and privacy boundaries documented in the project.

## After real use: review history retention first

The current app stores at most 100 shifts. If real riders find that limit restrictive, first gather consented, non-sensitive feedback about how long they need to keep records and how they currently export backups. Then propose a retention approach that keeps data-loss behavior explicit and maintains validated backup/restore paths. Do not silently delete old records or promise unlimited history.

## Other possible review topics

- Conduct a small, clearly described usability review before changing labels or workflow assumptions.
- Expand device and accessibility checks based on a defined acceptance plan.
- Revisit backup portability and user guidance based on observed use, without implying encryption or cloud sync that is not implemented.

No dates, adoption targets, performance gains, or commercial outcomes are promised.
